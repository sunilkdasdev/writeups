Gatlinburg - Great Smoky Mountains

Overview

Gatlinburg is a mountain town in Tennessee, gateway to the Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Known for its scenic beauty, outdoor adventures, and family-friendly attractions.

Top Attractions

  • Great Smoky Mountains National Park
  • Clingmans Dome - Highest point in the park
  • Cades Cove - Historic valley
  • Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail
  • Oconaluftee Indian Village

Things to Do

  • Hiking - Over 800 miles of trails
  • Wildlife viewing - Black bears, deer, elk
  • Scenic drives along Roaring Fork and Cades Cove Loop
  • Photography at sunrise/sunset spots
  • Exploring historic log cabins and mills

Best Hiking Trails

  • Alum Cave Trail to Clingmans Dome
  • Laurel Falls Trail
  • Trillium Gap Trail to Grotto Falls
  • Ramsey Cascades Trail
  • Andrews Bald - Best sunrise spot

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April-May) for wildflowers, Summer (June-August) for warm weather activities, Fall (September-November) for spectacular fall foliage, Winter (December-February) for fewer crowds and snow views.
