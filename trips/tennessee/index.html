Tennessee

Overview

Tennessee is a state in the southeastern United States. It is known for its music, whiskey, and stunning natural beauty, from the Great Smoky Mountains to the Mississippi River.

Major Cities

  • Nashville - Music City USA
  • Memphis - Home of Blues and Elvis
  • Knoxville - College town with mountain charm
  • Chattanooga - Outdoor adventure hub

Top Attractions

  • Great Smoky Mountains National Park
  • Grand Ole Opry
  • Graceland
  • Rocky Mount State Historic Site
  • Cades Cove
