San Francisco

Overview

San Francisco is a cultural and commercial center in Northern California. The city is known for its steep rolling hills, eclectic architecture, and iconic landmarks.

Iconic Landmarks

  • Golden Gate Bridge
  • Alcatraz Island
  • Fisherman's Wharf
  • Coit Tower
  • Painted Ladies

Neighborhoods

  • Chinatown - Oldest in North America
  • Haight-Ashbury - Historic hippie district
  • Mission District - Art and culture
  • Sausalito - Across the bay
