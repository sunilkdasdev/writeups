Toronto

Overview

Toronto is the capital city of the Canadian province of Ontario. With a population of 2.7 million, it is the most populous city in Canada and the fourth most populous city in North America.

Top Attractions

  • CN Tower
  • Royal Ontario Museum
  • Distillery District
  • Toronto Islands
  • St. Lawrence Market

Neighborhoods

  • Yorkville - Shopping and dining
  • Kensington Market - Eclectic vibe
  • Queen Street West - Art and fashion
  • The Beaches - Local beach community
