Bryson City

Overview

Bryson City is a town in Swain County, North Carolina. It is a charming mountain town known for its outdoor activities, scenic train rides, and access to the Great Smoky Mountains.

Top Attractions

  • Great Smoky Mountains Railroad
  • Deep Creek Tube Center
  • Fontana Dam
  • Clingmans Dome
  • Bryson City Scenic Trail

Things to Do

  • Hiking in the Smokies
  • Whitewater rafting
  • Fishing in mountain streams
  • Scenic train excursions
  • Exploring local shops and restaurants
