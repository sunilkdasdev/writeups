Canada

Overview

Canada is a country in North America. Its ten provinces and three territories extend from the Atlantic to the Pacific and northward to the Arctic Ocean, covering 9.98 million square kilometres, making it the world's second-largest country by total area.

Places to Visit

  • Niagara Falls
  • Banff National Park
  • CN Tower
  • Old Quebec
  • Stanley Park

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Canada is between June and August for warm weather, or December to March for winter activities.
